The Drop App

Five tabs.
One ecosystem.

No app like this exists. The Drop app is machine-agnostic — it works for any capsule machine, not just ours. That's how you become the category platform, not just a brand tool.

Machine Locator — Explore

Map-first discovery of capsule machines near you. Filter by series, rarity, venue type. Get notified when a new machine drops in your area. This tab is the beachhead — the utility hook that gets everyone in, collector or not.

Strategic model: Biter app — influencer content creates map pins, map pins drive foot traffic, foot traffic drives conversions.

Map-First Crowdsourced Machine-Agnostic

Collector Feed

UGC community for showing off your haul. Pull tab reveals, Secret tier unboxings, rare finds — all live here. This is where the viral loop lives. One Secret tier pull filmed and shared reaches thousands. The feed is the fuel.

Business Model

Multiple revenue
layers. One platform.

This is a three-sided marketplace: the Quarter Drop brand, the collector community, and independent makers. Each layer monetizes independently and strengthens the others.

01

Capsule Machine Revenue

Direct product sales from Quarter Drop branded machines. High margin physical product with recurring restocks per series drop.

02

Venue Placement Partnerships

Revenue share or placement fees with host venues. Venue gets foot traffic; we get premium real estate in cultural hubs.

03

App Marketplace Commission

Transaction fees on Makers marketplace sales. As the maker ecosystem grows, this becomes a scalable passive revenue layer.

04

Series Licensing / Collabs

Quarter Drop IP licensed to other artists, or collaboration drops with streetwear brands, anime properties, gaming studios.

05

App Premium / Data Layer

Collector premium tiers. Community data as placement intelligence. Long-term B2B opportunity selling machine placement insights to operators nationally.
